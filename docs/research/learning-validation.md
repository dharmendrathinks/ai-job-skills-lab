## Remaining evidence gates

- A frozen human-reviewed held-out skill set and measured precision/recall. The
  95%/85% values remain targets; the evaluator does not manufacture labels.
- Human comparison of connected paths, legacy briefs and the straightforward
  prompt baseline over identical supplied evidence.
- User selection of a real path and independently inspected implementation,
  failures, corrections and teaching outcomes. Draft generation is not progress.
- Stable complete collection history and renewed compatible source authorization
  before comparable longitudinal claims or continued retention beyond expiry.
- Native browser visual review; scripted behavior and escaped offline rendering
  checks do not establish a pixel-level result.
