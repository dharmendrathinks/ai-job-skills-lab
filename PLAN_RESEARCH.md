## 1. Product vision and boundaries

AI Job Radar will extend a fork of **MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search** into a personal engineering-research workspace.

Its central question is:

> What engineering problems are organizations hiring people to solve, and which are worth learning, building for, validating commercially, or explaining through a real experiment?

The primary user is Dharmendra: an engineer, open-source contributor, prospective product builder, and creator of Dharmendra Thinks. Other engineers can use the public fork with their own private research interests and evidence profiles.

The complete product connects:

**Configure interests → collect permitted evidence → inspect descriptions → analyze engineering capabilities → select a brief → build or validate → record outcomes → refresh.**

It produces four connected but independently assessed outputs:

1. Learning priorities with prerequisites and practical exercises.
2. Open-source project or contribution briefs.
3. Product and monetization hypotheses with validation plans.
4. YouTube engineering-experiment briefs.

Success means better decisions and useful completed work, supported by inspectable evidence. Listing volume, generated recommendation count, and keyword coverage are operational measures, not product success.

### Committed domain and experience

The default research domain includes:

- Applied AI and AI product engineering.
- LLM applications, agents, tools, and automation.
- Retrieval, knowledge systems, and data pipelines.
- Evaluation, reliability, observability, and security.
- AI infrastructure, inference, deployment, and relevant ML engineering.

Classify **responsibilities**, retaining titles as discovery and contextual evidence. Distinguish applied engineering, research-heavy work, mixed roles, and uncertain classifications. Research-heavy roles remain available as a separate analytical segment.

Research is global within measured source coverage. Geography, language, industry, seniority, workplace, and employment type are analytical dimensions. None becomes a default personal-eligibility exclusion.

Personalization follows market analysis. A role remains useful evidence when it is onsite, overseas, senior, contractual, outside the user’s application eligibility, or mentions capabilities the user already knows.

Broader IT-domain support is a committed extension capability in Phase 7, with separate domain configurations and evaluations. AI remains the default experience.

### Boundaries

- Job advertisements describe advertised expectations; they do not establish actual engineering practice, hiring completion, software purchasing demand, or audience demand.
- Preserve upstream’s application workflow as a distinct optional mode.
- Keep AI Trend Radar separate, with versioned file interchange.
- JobSpy is a read-only provider reference. Never install, import, wrap, vendor, or depend on it.
- No required paid job-data service or expiring trial: **job-data budget ₹0**.
- No additional spending beyond the existing Codex plan. Use subscription access within its allowances; no separately billed API worker, credit top-ups, paid fallback, additional subscription, hosting purchase, or hardware purchase. Record resource assumptions for proposed experiments separately; a brief never authorizes spending.
- No automatic applications, outreach, publishing, repository execution, or external writes.
- A hosted dashboard, SaaS infrastructure, distributed workers, vector database, or replacement application framework is not committed.

## 6. Global sources and trustworthy observations

### Source qualification policy

Each source registry entry references `SourcePolicy v1` and records the exact method, public documentation, usage review date, authentication, cost, allowed processing/hosted disclosure/retention/export, attribution, geography/language scope, and review status.

A source can be enabled only for the uses supported by its documentation or authorization. Access permission and historical retention can have different statuses.

- Successful access, robots allowance, public HTML, and an open-source collector license are insufficient on their own.
- Stop at access restrictions. No CAPTCHA bypass, borrowed credentials, identity rotation, proxy rotation, or restriction-evading browser impersonation.
- Research mode does not use an unapproved WebSearch or scraping fallback to route around a blocked collector.
- Source text supplied manually must also have usable provenance and a permitted processing basis; imports are not an access-policy loophole.
- Disable affected capabilities when permissions or availability change. Other sources and previously permitted work continue.

### Retention and withdrawal from first capture — Phase 2

Before enabling a source, implement usable storage, expiry, deletion, and export behavior for its intended acquisition and processing path. Unknown permission is not indefinite retention permission. This gate applies to collectors, manual imports, and P3 context evidence. Qualify hosted-model disclosure and retention as well as local storage; a permitted local capture does not automatically permit model submission.

Cover raw responses and descriptions, normalized text, excerpts, prompts and model outputs, requirements, evaluation labels, caches, aggregates, briefs, reports, temporary files, controlled exports, and backups. Record transitive dependencies even when a derived artifact contains no verbatim text. ### Identity, revisions, and vacancy state

- Source identity is `(provider, instance, board/feed, native_id)`; use a stable source URL identity only when native IDs are unavailable.

- Group cross-source openings only with strong evidence such as a shared authoritative requisition or validated canonical application identity.
- Similar employer/title/description creates a possible-duplicate relationship, not an automatic merge.
- Preserve grouping decisions, provenance, uncertainty, and reversibility.
- Same source ID with changed text creates a revision. A similar new ID is a possible repost. Reopening never resets original discovery history.
- Record source publication, source update, first discovery, acquisition, revalidation, and last positive availability independently.
- Preserve original country/language and normalized segments; multi-country counts must not inflate the overall opening total.

## 7. Central LLM analysis and four outputs

### Evidence pipeline

Keep four layers distinct:

| Layer | Meaning | Control |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Source statements** | What a captured description explicitly says | Exact revision and validated evidence span |
| **B. Normalization/classification** | Required/preferred modality, concepts, responsibility/domain labels, aliases | Versioned taxonomy and transformation provenance |
| **C. Cross-source inference** | Recurring problem patterns and plausible engineering needs | Linked supporting/contradicting observations and uncertainty |
| **D. Recommendations** | Learning, contribution, product, or experiment proposals | Separate rubric, assumptions, alternatives, and review |

Extract responsibilities, required/preferred skills, engineering capabilities, tools, operational constraints, and contextual requirements from captured descriptions.

Every explicit claim references a span in the captured text. Preserve source representation: an aggregator-transformed description is not falsely described as an untouched employer document. Provider skill tags and enrichment are separately labeled.

Titles, stored gap bullets, industry labels, and company categories cannot establish explicit requirements. Missing or ambiguous requirements stay unknown.

Distinguish capability from implementation: retrieval evaluation is a capability; a named framework is one implementation choice. Keep many-to-many mappings and avoid inflating demand through synonyms.

Use deterministic code for identities, counts, deduplication, persistence, span validation, denominators, and report calculations. Aggregate each capability once per opening, and separately once per employer; show required and preferred occurrences independently.

### Evaluation design

- Build development and held-out sets grouped by employer and duplicate-opening family to prevent leakage.
- Include applied AI, research-heavy, mixed and non-AI roles; required/preferred ambiguity; missing descriptions; non-English text; onsite/overseas/contract roles; and repeated syndication.

- Keep challenge fixtures separate from operational prevalence estimates.

Targets are proposals, not results. Failure can require improving a capability, narrowing a claim, or withholding a recommendation. It must not be hidden by lowering denominators.

Retain upstream Python tests, Bun fixture tests, skill lint, security guards, version checks, and application smoke checks. Add semantic workflow replay and human evaluation: tests checking Markdown contains certain phrases cannot establish research accuracy.

CI remains network-free for ordinary portal tests. Live validation uses isolated private state and requires source qualification. Application regression scenarios include tracker exclusion, original fit bands, deadline handling, gap persistence, legacy fields, application archives, and no accidental research writes.
